# Design brief: Sri GVS Sankaraiah Memorial High School

- **Design read:** A welcoming, ambitious Gudur school for families and students in Classes 1–10, with a trustworthy school front door and an unusually capable, approachable digital office.
- **Concept spine:** The School of Possibility. A continuous camera move approaches a fictional, aspirational school campus at sunrise, then moves toward its entry courtyard and learning spaces to signal welcome, growth and care.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema. The hero film is controlled by visitor scroll and supports reduced-motion fallback.
- **Locked palette:** Royal navy #12315A, school blue #1F5E9C, chalk white #F7FAFE, deep ink #15263A, and a single warm saffron-gold accent #D5A640. The school requested bold colour and the gold accent is restrained to emblems, focus states and small highlights.
- **Locked type:** Outfit for display and Inter for body/interface. Rounded geometric headings feel student-friendly; Inter keeps administrative tables highly readable.
- **Animation mode:** animated-website
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One continuous ~15-second camera move keeps the school as one cohesive subject and avoids jarring seams while the semantic chapters change over it.
- **Journey:** (1) Arrival: a wide sunrise view of the fictional school building, headline “A brighter future starts here”. (2) Welcome: the camera glides toward the main entrance, headline “Every child belongs”. (3) Learning: the move reveals a courtyard with students arriving in uniform, headline “Learn with confidence”. (4) Partnership: the camera settles into a beautiful campus view, headline “School and family, together”.
- **World grammar:** cinematic, photorealistic fictional Andhra Pradesh school campus, royal navy and chalk white details with restrained saffron-gold accents, soft early morning sunlight from camera-left, natural trees and clean grounds, warm human scale, realistic Indian architecture, slow stable forward dolly, no cuts, no shake, no flicker, no on-screen text or logos.
- **Mobile framing:** keep the building entrance and central campus in the center-safe area; the scroll engine will use a lighter mobile encode and matching first-frame poster.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop film under 32 MiB; mobile film under 16 MiB.
- **Section plan:** (1) hero scroll journey; (2) asymmetric quick-access grid; (3) split school story; (4) horizontal notice ticker and circular list; (5) student life image feature; (6) login access directory; (7) contact/footer band. After login, role dashboards use a separate responsive admin shell.
- **Asset plan:** custom vector campus artwork rendered as a 15-second continuous zoom film with exact desktop/mobile posters; custom unofficial crest; campus section visual; 3:2 launch cover and OG card; favicon, Apple touch icon and manifest icons. Photorealistic image generation was unavailable because the connected generation workspace had no credits, so the hero uses a clearly illustrative campus rendering.
- **CTA inventory:** “Explore school” scrolls to school story; “School login” opens role selector; “View notices” opens circulars; “Open dashboard” enters the demo role portal; “Mark attendance” is a teacher-only simulated action; “Publish homework” is teacher-only demo action; “Record marks” is teacher-only demo action; “Review reports” opens school overview; “Contact school” opens contact details.
- **Interaction safety:** use fictional student/staff/guardian identities and non-dialable demo contact labels. Attendance, parent SMS, payments, camera/face and geofence are demonstrators until secure authentication, school-approved data, payment keys, messaging provider and location controls are configured.
- **Accessibility:** responsive layouts, keyboard-visible focus, descriptive labels, semantic headings, clear demo-data notices, and reduced-motion support.
- **Identity axes:** royal-blue institutional palette; Outfit + Inter type; continuous school-campus camera journey; scroll-controlled film; distinct rectangular school-office CTA and circular login selector; medium-radius corners with fine blue-gray borders.
